About PDF Weave

Merging PDFs should be simple.

PDF Weave is a free, fast and private way to combine PDF files into one document — with control over exactly which pages make it into the final file.

Why PDF Weave?

Fast & free

No subscriptions, no watermarks, no waiting. Your files merge in seconds — right from your browser.

Private by design

Your PDFs are processed and then deleted automatically. Merged files expire shortly after you download them.

Page-level control

Choose which pages to include from every file — 2-5, 1,3,8, or everything at once.

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload your PDFs

    Drag & drop up to 10 files, or click to browse. Arrange them in the order you want.

  2. 2

    Choose your pages

    Optional: enter a page range per file, like 2-5 or 1,3, to keep only what you need.

  3. 3

    Merge & download

    Hit Merge, review the result on the thank-you page, then click the download button to save merged.pdf.
